# ConsuCare — User Guide

ConsuCare works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Visit https://consu-care.vercel.app to use the hosted site.

On a phone, tap the **☰** button at the top right to open the menu.

## Getting started

1. Open the site and choose **Get Started**.
2. Pick **Patient** or **Supporter**, then enter your name, email, community and a password. Supporters provide their condition and diagnosis year and choose whether to share those health details.
3. Patients go straight to their hub. Supporters can sign in straight away, but appear in Discover only after an admin approves them and only while health-detail sharing is enabled.

## For patients

| Screen | What you can do |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Patient Hub** | See active supporters, care goals and unread messages |
| **Discover Supporters** | Search the approved directory by name, condition, community or management strategies |
| **Supporter profile** | Read shared lived-experience details and reviews; leave a review or request support |
| **Support request** | Choose a care focus, preferred frequency and describe the practical or emotional peer support you are seeking |
| **Support Network** | Active, Pending and Past tabs; message a supporter or view care goals |
| **Care Goals** | Add a goal with milestones and track progress. A goal completes when every milestone is ticked |
| **Messages** | Chat with your supporters in real time |
| **Notifications** | Request updates, new messages and completed milestones, grouped into Today and Earlier; **Mark all read** |
| **Settings** | Edit your profile, notification preferences and password |

## For supporters

| Screen | What you can do |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Supporter Hub** | See pending requests and **Accept** or **Decline** them; follow supported patients' goal progress |
| **Messages** | Chat with connected patients in real time |
| **Notifications** | New requests and messages |
| **Settings** | Edit your profile and preferences |

## For admins

**Supporter Verification** lists supporter applications. Filter by All / Pending / Approved / Rejected, then **Approve** or **Reject**. Only approved supporters who opted in to sharing are shown in Discover.

## Tips

- Refreshing the page keeps you signed in.
- To see live chat, sign in as the patient in one window and the supporter in a private window, then send messages between them.
- Supporter health information is private by default and can be hidden later in Settings. ConsuCare is peer support, not medical advice or a substitute for a care team.
- If the first load is slow, give it a moment — the server may be starting up after a quiet period.
